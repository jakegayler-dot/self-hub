CrossFit percentiles

% of the field you beat. Lifts, pull-ups, runs and benchmark WODs are against male CrossFitters' self-reported 2019 Open profiles. That pool skews optimistic and isn't bodyweight-adjusted, so read yours as slightly conservative. Rows are against Concept2 2025 lightweight men, and the Open is your rank in your division. Lifts use your best e1RM in 60 days (12 months if nothing recent); everything else uses your best Rx result in 12 months. The bodyweight-ratio level is shown alongside.
