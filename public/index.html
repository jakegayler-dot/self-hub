Weekly training load

ACWR = this week's load ÷ your own trailing 4-week average — 0.8–1.3 is a sustainable range (standard sports-science heuristic, not a precise measure); below it you're detraining relative to your recent normal, above 1.5 is a real spike. Early in the week this reads low simply because the week isn't over yet — that's expected, not a shortfall.

Strength profile

Best estimated 1RM (Epley) for each benchmark lift, scored against a rough "typical intermediate" ratio for its pattern — 1.0 means about typical, not "strong" in absolute terms. This makes hinge and vertical-pull comparable even though a 2x-bodyweight deadlift and a 2x-bodyweight pull-up aren't the same rarity. Lowest bars are your current weak points relative to what's normal for that pattern. Still rough — the standards are general ballparks, not tailored to you, and patterns are a simplification.
