CrossFit percentiles

How this is worked out

% of the field you beat. Lifts, pull-ups, runs and benchmark WODs are against male CrossFitters' self-reported 2019 Open profiles. That pool skews optimistic and isn't bodyweight-adjusted, so read yours as slightly conservative. Rows are against Concept2 2025 lightweight men, and the Open is your rank in your division. Each uses your best ever logged (the field's numbers are lifetime bests too): a lift's best single or e1RM (easy sets under RPE 7 don't count), your most pull-ups, your fastest Rx time. If that best is over a year old, your best of the last 12 months (60 days for lifts) shows under it. Strict pull-ups are against StrengthLevel lifters at your bodyweight. Level is shown only where a real standard exists: lifts by bodyweight ratio, strict pull-ups by reps at your bodyweight, rows by Concept2's lightweight class.
